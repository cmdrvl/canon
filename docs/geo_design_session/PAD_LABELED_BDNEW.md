PAD resolver predicate:

- Inputs are both parsed `NUMBER` + `STREET` and leading house-number/range extracted from raw `PROPERTY_ADDRESS`, borough-scoped by `COUNTY_FIPS`.
- Street normalization: uppercase, non-alphanumeric to spaces, strip ordinal suffixes, expand `W/E/N/S` to full directionals, expand `ST` to `STREET`, `AV`/`AVE` to `AVENUE`, collapse spaces.
- House-number handling: exact display match to PAD low/high display, or numeric interval overlap against `LOW_HOUSE_NUMBER_INT`/`HIGH_HOUSE_NUMBER_INT`; hyphenates with a shorter second component are treated literally as one concatenated Queens-style number; normal ranges such as `241-249` remain ranges.
- Parity: structured probe showed `PARITY='1'` is odd, `PARITY='2'` is even, `PARITY='0'`/NULL is accepted as both/unknown. Numeric matches require parity unless the input itself is a range.
- Street join path uses `PAD_SND_HOT` by `STREET_NAME_NORMALIZED` or `FULL_STREET_NAME_NORMALIZED` to `STREET_CODE_BORO`, with direct `PAD_ADDRESS_HOT.STREET_NAME_NORMALIZED` fallback.
- Point-grain fan-out is retained. `MULTI_BBL` means a point resolved to more than one distinct PAD BBL; no first/limit collapse was used.

## Result

PAD is sparse and not a gross-error recovery mechanism under this point-grain predicate. When PAD resolves in the already-correct class, it mostly confirms the right lot; when it resolves in the gross class, it mostly confirms the wrong PIP lot.

## Q1b PAD_BBL Condo Crosswalk

SQL receipt: `Q1B_MISSING_GEOMETRY_SQL` below.

Primary Q1b predicate: `condo_representation_residue` points with zero MapPLUTO-present ACRIS truth BBLs. For each such point, select one deterministic missing ACRIS condo unit BBL as `MIN(unit_bbl)`, map it through `PAD_BBL_HOT` where `unit_bbl BETWEEN LOW_BBL_KEY AND HIGH_BBL_KEY`, and compare `BILLING_BBL_KEY` to the geometry PIP BBL(s) at the point.

| grain | denominator | with PAD_BBL row | with billing BBL | billing equals PIP | distinct units with billing | points with any billing equals PIP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| selected_unit_bbl_grain | 24 | 24 | 24 | 10 | 24 | n/a |
| selected_point_grain | 31 | 31 | 31 | 10 | n/a | 10 |

The selected point denominator reproduces the requested 31 missing-geometry condo population. The distinct selected-unit denominator is 24 because the same selected unit BBL can appear on more than one point row.
